Description
The YMCAs of Quebec are a charitable organization whose mission is to build stronger communities by providing opportunities for development and an active, healthy and engaged life for all. Its ten YMCA centers, its International Language School, its Ski School, its Residence, its Kanawana YMCA Camp and its 43 service points across the province are visited each year by more than 130,000 people. The first YMCA in North America was founded in Montreal in November 1851. The YMCAs of Quebec are part of a Canadian network that includes 50 YMCA associations. Mission
The Scout Movement is an educational movement based on volunteerism. The aim of Scouting is to contribute to the development of young people by helping them to realize their full potential as people, according to the principles set out by founder Lord Robert Baden-Powell. It is a non-political movement, open to all, without distinction of origin or belief.
Description
Activities
Weekly evening meetings from September to June
Winter camp: one weekend for each unit
Summer camp: one weekend for each unit
Various outings and activities throughout the year
Costs
Annual contribution depending on the units.
Scouting is an educational movement open to all. It encourages young people to take charge of their own progress. The objective is to allow young people to develop all facets of their personality (physical, social, emotional, intellectual and spiritual) through play, teamwork, nature, commitment, and celebration. This is so that he later becomes an autonomous adult, capable of engaging in society with the aim of improving it.
Young people are supervised by responsible adults trained to guide them, support them and lead them in all their efforts.
In each unit the number of young people is limited in order to respect a facilitator/young person ration of 1/8. Even though the Scout year goes from September to September, registrations are done at any time and, depending on the availability of places in the unit, a young person can start at any time of the year. Each young person is entitled to a trial period of three meetings.
