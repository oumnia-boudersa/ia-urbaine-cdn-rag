Mission
Improve the quality of life of retirees by promoting mutual assistance, social inclusion and access to adapted services. Our goal is to support and promote the contribution of seniors to the Montreal community, by offering enriching activities and defending their rights. Join us in being a strong voice for retirees and creating a welcoming and supportive environment for all seniors.
Description
The Alliance of Associations of Retirees and Seniors of Quebec, based in Montreal, is a community organization committed to the well-being of retirees and seniors in the region. Our mission is to accompany and support this precious community through various activities and services adapted to their needs.
Specializing in the areas of retirement and retiree associations, our organization implements initiatives aimed at improving the quality of life of seniors, fostering social connections and promoting physical and mental well-being.
Our interventions revolve around the organization of meetings, workshops and conferences on various themes such as health, prevention, culture and leisure. We also encourage the social and civic engagement of retirees through solidarity actions and community projects.
Organization of cultural and recreational events
Prevention and awareness workshops
Solidarity and voluntary actions
In the heart of the city of Montreal, the Alliance of Associations of Retirees and Seniors of Quebec is a key player in local community life, working for the well-being and development of seniors. Join us in this great collective adventure dedicated to the better life of retirees and seniors in our beautiful region!
