Mission
Action by Christians for the Abolition of Torture (ACAT) is the Canadian branch of an international federation, FIACAT, with consultative status at the UN. ACAT Canada is a national ecumenical organization that has advocated for human rights for nearly 40 years. She intervenes especially in cases of torture and the death penalty. It informs through awareness and action campaigns, intervenes through letters of protest from its members to the authorities concerned, educates about human rights, invites spiritual reflection and above all, ensures that situations of mistreatment still existing in Canada are reported to the UN. Its head office is in Montreal and its activities take place in French, but it has members across Canada.
Description
Advocacy, Human rights education, Spirituality, Support for healing victims of torture

Mission
The mission of the Corporation of Master Electricians of Quebec (CMEQ) is to protect the public by verifying the qualification, probity and competence of its members. It regulates and disciplines the professional conduct of electrical contractors, providing safety and expertise to the community.
Description
Corporation of Master Electricians of Quebec
About the CMEQ
Since 1950, the Corporation of Master Electricians of Quebec (CMEQ) has protected the public by verifying and controlling the qualification of its members to guarantee their integrity and competence. It regulates and disciplines the professional conduct of electrical contractors.
Services offered
Verification of member qualifications
Regulation and professional discipline
Information on electrical safety laws and practices
Continuing education for electrical contractors
General public
The CMEQ protects the public by verifying the qualifications of its members and providing information on electrical safety laws and practices.
Electrical Contractors
The CMEQ develops practical tools and technical, legal and administrative information documents to support its members in their practice of the profession.
Services offered to entrepreneurs
Tools and calculators
Reference documents
Partnerships with Hydro-Québec and others
Specific training for the operation of a subcategory 16 license
Continuing education
The CMEQ offers a range of training for electrical contractors, adapted to the operation of a subcategory 16 license, as well as general training useful for managing a construction company.
Center of Expertise and Training (CEF)
The CMEQ CEF offers a portal for members to access training and declare their continuing education hours. Respondents carrying out construction work must complete 16 hours of training over a period of 24 months.

Description
Founded in 2009, Support for Caregivers is a non-profit organization funded by the Government of Quebec and the Lucie and André Chagnon Family. The main mandate of Support for Caregivers consists of supporting the deployment of information, training, psychosocial support and respite services, through funding organizations present in the communities. L’Appui also acts as a mobilization and consultation agent for the various stakeholders who are dedicated to supporting caregivers and who participate in improving their quality of life. Mission
The mission of the Black League of Quebec is to defend human rights, fight against injustice and discrimination, and offer support to people who are victims of racism and racial profiling. We are committed to promoting equality of opportunity and opportunity for all citizens, raising awareness among governments and society of the importance of respecting fundamental rights.
Description
The Quebec Negro League
The Black League of Quebec is a non-profit organization founded in 1969 to defend human rights and social justice. We serve all cultural communities who fight to obtain impartial justice, covering not only Quebec but all of Canada.
Our Services
Defense of human rights daily for the black and cultural community.
Legal clinic offering free services.
Youth Integration Project to help young people aged 16 to 30 enter the job market.
The Black League of Quebec is the only organization to offer a daily human rights service to people from the black and cultural community. We are here to help you if you are a victim of racism, discrimination, or any other injustice.
About Us
The Quebec Black League works to raise the moral, social, political and economic consciousness of Canada. We denounce the injustices and discrimination of the vicious system by being the spokesperson for the voiceless, the oppressed and the victims. If your rights have been violated or violated, do not hesitate to contact us.
Our objectives include the defense of community rights, popular education to respect citizen rights, raising government awareness of the importance of equal opportunities, encouraging citizen participation, and defending victims of injustice and abuse of power.
Since 1969, The Black League of Quebec has established links with other organizations to better serve the community. We refer certain complaints to the appropriate authorities and have created specialized organizations to respond to the specific needs of the community.

Mission
The Elizabeth Fry Society of Quebec is a community organization founded in 1977. Its mission is to help women who are in trouble with the law, who have already had them or who are at risk of having them. She ensures that women's rights are respected and that their conditions of detention are improved.
Description
The values that support our action are: accountability, respect for oneself and others, autonomy, affirmation and honesty.
We aim to:
promote the social integration of women in the justice system, in a supportive and supervisory environment;
meet their needs by offering them specialized services adapted to their economic and social situations;
defend their interests and raise awareness among governments and public opinion of their reality;
raise awareness among the various community resources of their needs in order to maximize the services offered to them.
