Description
À Deux Mains / Head & Hands is a community organization that has supported Montreal youth since 1970. Our mission is to empower young people and provide them with a safe space to access a variety of essential services.
At Head & Hands, we believe in creating a society where all young people can participate and be inspired by the endless possibilities available to them. We offer a range of programs and services adapted to young people aged 12 to 25, regardless of their origin or insurance status.
Medical services: Our clinic offers medical services to young people aged 12 to 25, with or without health insurance. For more information, contact us at clinic@headandhands.ca.
Legal services: We offer a free legal clinic for clients of all ages, as well as individual support for young people aged 12 to 25. Our legal coordinators are commissioners of oaths. Contact us at legalclinic@headandhands.ca.
Psychosocial support: Our free consultation services are youth-oriented, non-judgmental and flexible in duration. Offered to young people aged 12 to 25, regardless of their neighborhood. Email counselingdirector@headandhands.ca for support.
Young Parents Program (PJP): The PJP is a space for parents and future parents under 25 to meet. We offer regular workshops, activities and outings. Contact ypp_pjp@headandhands.ca for more details.
Front-line services: Our services include harm reduction, food services and various resources for young people aged 12 to 25. No ID or proof of address required. For more information, write to bonjour@headandhands.ca.
Youth 2000 (J2K): Our youth center located in Décarie is a space for adolescents aged 12 to 17 to relax, participate in activities and workshops. Contact jeunesse2000@headandhands.ca with any questions.
For any questions or to contact us, write to us at info@headandhands.ca or call us at (514) 481-0277. We are here to support you and provide you with the resources you need.
Head & Hands has been working to empower young people in Montreal for over 50 years. Our services, peer education and community initiatives aim to help young people make informed decisions about their physical and mental well-being.
