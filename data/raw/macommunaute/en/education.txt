Mission
Parents Engaged for Early Childhood's mission is to offer, develop and support, particularly in Quebec, programs mainly at home, aimed at improving the ability of immigrant parents and those from disadvantaged backgrounds to strengthen their children's ability to learn at a young age as well as to better prepare them for school.
Description
Program
Activities are offered at each visit, in the families' homes, 30 weeks a year by home workers. Home visits are made every week or two, depending on the needs of the parents.
  The activities, both fun and educational, are focused on the overall development of the child. Parents become familiar with observation and skill development through play and daily activities around the house.
  The worker brings a new book into the family, so that parent and child enjoy exploring a variety of books and establish a reading routine at home. Thus, the parent is made aware of the importance of exposing their child to reading to support them in their language development in particular.
  The targeted skills work on language, the development of fine and gross motor skills, memory and listening skills, as well as emotional development and hand-eye coordination.
  In addition, parents can discuss parenting techniques with the counselor and we also do referrals.
Group activities
Group activities on different subjects and outings are organized for parents.
