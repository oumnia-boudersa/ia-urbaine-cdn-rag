Description
Building on more than 150 years of excellent community service, the Ometz Agency was created in April 2008. It is the result of the merger of Jewish Employment Montreal (JEM), Jewish Family Services (JFS) and Jewish Immigrant Assistance Services (JIAS). The Ometz Agency offers a whole range of integrated services relating to all stages of life. We are committed to strengthening those who are going through difficult times in their lives.
In 1863, when Montreal had just under 1,000 Jews, a group of community leaders met in a store on Rue Saint-Jacques and created the Young Men’s Hebrew Benevolent Society. Little did they know that they were founding what would later become Jewish Family Services (JFS).
The growing wave of immigrants to Canada, in part due to the many newcomers who were fleeing their countries and who risked being deported upon their arrival, justified the creation of the Jewish Immigrant Assistance Services (JIAS) in 1922. Over the years, the JIAS helped hundreds of thousands of Jewish immigrants in their immigration application process and in establishing themselves in Canada.
In 1945, Jewish Employment Montreal (JEM) was founded to meet the needs of Canadian war veterans and people arriving from Europe in search of work. Through the variety of services offered to job seekers and employers, JEM has become a Montreal leader in employment and placement.
Currently, there are more than 13,000 people per year who contact the Ometz Agency in order to improve their quality of life. The Agency offers a range of services for the whole family, whether it concerns difficulties experienced following the breakdown of the family or mental illness, financial issues, looking for a job or settling in Montreal.

Mission
The Immigrant Worker Center (CTI) defends the rights of immigrants in their workplaces and fights for dignity, respect and justice. Some of our main objectives are: • Promote popular education, particularly regarding workers' rights • Improve working conditions for immigrant workers • Organize mobilization around problems related to working conditions, including for example: workplace accidents, harassment, unpaid wages, overtime, maternity leave, etc. • Provide a space that allows immigrant workers to receive information, resources, and references. We offer these resources and references in several languages ​​(English, French, Spanish, Hindi, Arabic, Iranian, Bengali, etc.). Join us to learn about your rights, acquire the tools that will allow you to improve your conditions at work, and to promote a movement for economic and social justice.

Mission
Club Ami is a non-profit community organization whose mission is to improve the quality of life of people struggling with mental health problems. Club Ami aims to develop an inclusive living environment by promoting the exchange and sharing of unique experiences. In addition, the organization encourages freedom of speech, artistic and intellectual expression while providing a place for participation in local and community integration.
Description
Club Ami is located in the Côte-des-Neiges district, in Montreal, and allows people in psychological suffering to have a space of their own, where it is possible to share moments of respite, a coffee or the exceptional presence of our members. We offer a variety of group activities: painting workshops, yoga, museum visits, conferences, and more.
The speakers and our director organize the activities, ensure the well-being of everyone within the organization and are available to hear and refer if necessary.
