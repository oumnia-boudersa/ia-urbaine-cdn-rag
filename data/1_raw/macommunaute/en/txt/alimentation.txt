Description

To increase access to healthy food for low-income community members, in a dignified and respectful manner.
To reduce the social isolation of participants and strengthen their connection with various sources of support.

Mission
The mission of the Côte-des-Neiges Community Development Corporation (CDC de CDN) is to bring together community organizations in the Côte-des-Neiges district, in order to promote solidarity and consultation among them with the aim of improving the quality and living conditions of the population, and to fight against poverty, discrimination and all forms of exclusion.
Description
OUR OBJECTIVES:
Internally
Facilitate the circulation of information, complementarity, mutual assistance and consultation between member organizations;
Within its means, act as a resource for organizations that express the need, for example in terms of structuring, democratic functioning, and fundraising;
In the neighborhood
Initiate or support initiatives in the community aimed at improving the social and/or economic conditions of neighborhood residents;
Initiate or support initiatives aimed at bringing together different cultural communities and contributing to the fight against all discrimination, exclusion and marginalization;
Outside
Defend the interests of the community and act as spokesperson for its members before various government, public or private authorities;
Maintain links with organizations pursuing similar goals, in order to fuel the work of the CDC and its members and maximize the impact of their interventions.

Mission
• Offer meals and a food emergency service to low-income people in the Côte-des-Neiges/Snowdon neighborhood. • Promote the development of skills of low-income people and facilitate their integration into the environment. • Defend the rights of disadvantaged people and raise community awareness of problems related to poverty.
Description
The MultiCaf Community Cafeteria, founded in 1986, is a non-profit organization which aims to help disadvantaged people in the Côte-des-Neiges/Snowdon district.
Our community cafeteria is open to everyone from Monday to Friday where 260 hot meals as well as breakfast are served daily. Our food assistance service provides some 750 families with balanced food baskets every month according to the recommendations of the Canadian Food Guide, all year round.
The ideal of the founders of MultiCaf was not limited to food assistance. They were convinced that the problems linked to poverty required the organization to go further. MultiCaf was to become a meeting place where disadvantaged people could come together to avoid isolation and together demand a just and equitable society. To this end, MultiCaf offers a large program of complementary activities such as food workshops and other relevant subjects, recreational outings, a monthly newspaper (written by our members), etc.

Mission
The Lunch Box Workshops, a project led by the Action 6-12 Committee since 2003, aims to educate healthy eating and food security for families in Notre-Dame-de-Grâce. As part of this project, children from primary and secondary schools, as well as neighborhood families, participate in free educational cooking workshops in conjunction with the community centers of Notre-Dame-de-Grâce.
Description
The Lunch Box Workshops is a program that aims to provide education on healthy eating and food security for young people and families in Notre-Dame-de-Grâce by carrying out free cooking and nutrition activities in community centers. As part of this program, children from elementary and secondary schools participate in educational workshops where they prepare the contents of a healthy lunch box. Families also have the opportunity to meet in neighborhood community centers to prepare and share meals.
OUR OBJECTIVES
Improve the food security of young people and their families by:
making it easier to prepare and share free, nutritious meals;
supporting participants in their learning of the relationship between food, health, the economy, the sustainable environment, and cultural traditions.
Enhance self-esteem and autonomy by:
developing knowledge, know-how and skills related to food;
promoting the development of social skills, strengthening the feeling of belonging and developing a sense of cooperation;
ensuring discovery and openness to different cultures through learning to cook.
encouraging the adoption of healthy lifestyle habits.
Support parents by:
providing opportunities to break their isolation, strengthen their sense of belonging, develop better support networks, and establish closer links with community services;
creating collaboration spaces that facilitate their integration into the community;
reducing barriers to integration into the labor market by providing places to practice the French language as well as offering work experience opportunities;
developing a better knowledge of attitudes promoting healthy eating and health.
OUR MAIN ACTIVITIES
Extracurricular cooking workshops for children in 4th and 5th grade of primary school.
Extracurricular cooking workshops for high school students.
Family cultural cooking workshops.
Summer picnics for families.
A Cooking Meetings program for parents.

Mission
MultiCaf's mission is to provide food aid, create social connections and seek resources to support vulnerable people in Côte-des-Neiges. By promoting food autonomy and sustainable development, we help fight food insecurity and social isolation.
Description
MultiCaf is a non-profit organization located in Côte-des-Neiges, Montreal, QC, Canada. Since its founding in 1989, MultiCaf has been committed to providing food assistance, creating social connections and seeking resources for all vulnerable people in the region.
In response to the current food insecurity crisis, MultiCaf offers a variety of services to support the community. The programs offered aim to meet the urgent needs of low-income people, newcomers to Quebec and anyone going through a difficult financial period.
The services offered by MultiCaf include:
6 internal and external food services
3 social services supported by dedicated workers
Agricultural projects developed since 2022 thanks to the INARI Network
In addition to providing economical meals and food emergency services, MultiCaf also offers a space dedicated to nutrition and healthy eating, thus aiming to promote food autonomy and sustainable development within the community.
Thanks to its dedicated team, MultiCaf is able to offer advice, facilitate the search for resources and direct members to other organizations capable of meeting their specific needs. The organization regularly collaborates with universities and institutions to conduct research projects on social issues such as homelessness, housing, nutrition and urban agriculture.
With nearly 35 years of experience, MultiCaf is well anchored in the Côte-des-Neiges community. Its commitment to food security and social well-being makes it a key player in the fight against food insecurity and social isolation.
If you are looking for food assistance, social support or simply a place to get involved as a volunteer, do not hesitate to contact MultiCaf. Together, we can help strengthen the food security and social fabric of our community.
