Mission
JOIN OUR TEAM AND HELP TRANSFORM LIVES Agence Ometz is a charitable organization that supports and encourages individuals and families by offering a wide range of social, educational, employment and immigration services. Our work is guided by our fundamental values: autonomy, inclusion, human dignity, supportive communities and excellence. We are dedicated to helping those who seek our support to reach their full potential and improve their quality of life. By joining Agence Ometz, you will work with a team of professionals who are compassionate, engaged and inspiring. Apply today!
Description
Building on more than 150 years of excellent community service, the Ometz Agency was created in April 2008. It is the result of the merger of Jewish Employment Montreal (JEM), Jewish Family Services (JFS) and Jewish Immigrant Assistance Services (JIAS). The Ometz Agency offers a whole range of integrated services relating to all stages of life. We are committed to strengthening those who are going through difficult times in their lives.
In 1863, when Montreal had just under 1,000 Jews, a group of community leaders met in a store on Rue Saint-Jacques and created the Young Men’s Hebrew Benevolent Society. Little did they know that they were founding what would later become Jewish Family Services (JFS).
The growing wave of immigrants to Canada, in part due to the many newcomers who were fleeing their countries and who risked being deported upon their arrival, justified the creation of the Jewish Immigrant Assistance Services (JIAS) in 1922. Over the years, the JIAS helped hundreds of thousands of Jewish immigrants in their immigration application process and in establishing themselves in Canada.
In 1945, Jewish Employment Montreal (JEM) was founded to meet the needs of Canadian war veterans and people arriving from Europe in search of work. Through the variety of services offered to job seekers and employers, JEM has become a Montreal leader in employment and placement.
Currently, there are more than 13,000 people per year who contact the Ometz Agency in order to improve their quality of life. The Agency offers a range of services for the whole family, whether it concerns difficulties experienced following the breakdown of the family or mental illness, financial issues, looking for a job or settling in Montreal.

Mission
Parents Engaged for Early Childhood's mission is to offer, develop and support, particularly in Quebec, programs mainly at home, aimed at improving the ability of immigrant parents and those from disadvantaged backgrounds to strengthen their children's ability to learn at a young age as well as to better prepare them for school.
Description
Program
Activities are offered at each visit, in the families' homes, 30 weeks a year by home workers. Home visits are made every week or two, depending on the needs of the parents.
  The activities, both fun and educational, are focused on the overall development of the child. Parents become familiar with observation and skill development through play and daily activities around the house.
  The worker brings a new book into the family, so that parent and child enjoy exploring a variety of books and establish a reading routine at home. Thus, the parent is made aware of the importance of exposing their child to reading to support them in their language development in particular.
  The targeted skills work on language, the development of fine and gross motor skills, memory and listening skills, as well as emotional development and hand-eye coordination.
  In addition, parents can discuss parenting techniques with the counselor and we also do referrals.
Group activities
Group activities on different subjects and outings are organized for parents.

Description
C.A.R.E Youth
The Center for Friendship, Resources and Mutual Aid for Youth (C.A.R.E Jeunesse) is a bilingual, non-profit provincial organization dedicated to cultivating a community and a voice for young people in care and formerly placed people.
For most young people, the transition to adulthood can be difficult. However, for the 2,000 young people who stop being taken care of each year in Quebec, this transition is even more complex. This is where C.A.R.E Jeunesse comes in.
Founded by former placements who themselves experienced the system, our organization's mission is to help these young adults after their departure. We aim to improve their living conditions, provide them with a supportive community and help them build a better future.
When a young adult who leaves child care finds himself facing difficulties, whether it be finding accommodation, food, help with administrative procedures, or simply someone with whom to share the holidays, he can count on C.A.R.E Jeunesse to establish these links and offer him support adapted to his needs.

Located in the Côte-des-Neiges—Notre-Dame-de-Grâce district, this organization offers affordable accommodation, allowing families to be closer to their children while reducing the financial stress often associated with health care.
This initiative is particularly beneficial for parents from remote regions, located more than 55 km from Montreal. By providing a comfortable and accessible place to stay, the Ronald McDonald House ensures that families can focus on their child's well-being, without being distracted by logistical concerns.
Services offered by the Ronald McDonald House include:
Affordable temporary accommodation for families
Proximity to Montreal hospitals, facilitating daily visits
Warm and welcoming environment for families
Emotional support and resources to help parents
Le Manoir aims to improve the quality of life of families in times of crisis, by providing them with a place where they can feel safe and supported. In addition to accommodation, the organization is committed to creating a support network between parents, thus promoting exchanges of experiences and bonds of mutual assistance.
In conclusion, the Ronald McDonald House is much more than just a place to stay. It is a space where families can find comfort and solidarity during difficult times. Thanks to its strategic location in Montreal and its commitment to families, this organization plays a crucial role in the care pathway for sick children.
